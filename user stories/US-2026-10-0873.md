# US-2026-10-0873

## Blocking after a VO rate change

When a VO changes the rate of an invoiced Construction Contract item, split the original and replacement rows.

Keep Retroactive Change Rate blocking behavior unchanged. Apply the allowance rules below to that VO type too.

Set the original item's blocking flags from its paid and progress percentages:

| Original item state | `block_item` | `temp_block_item` |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paid percent and progress percent both equal 100% | 1 | 0 |
| Either percentage does not equal 100% | 0 | 1 |

## Complete settlement through invoicing

Allow the temporarily blocked original item to be invoiced to complete its paid and progress percentages.

After an invoice updates the item, check both percentages again.

When both equal 100%, set `block_item = 1` and `temp_block_item = 0` on the original item.

Otherwise, keep `block_item = 0` and `temp_block_item = 1`.

## Preserve the allowance transfer

Capture the original item's stored `over_billing_allowance_quantity` before splitting or clearing its allowance.

Transfer that allowance once to the active replacement. The replacement allowance covers the original total quantity, not only the remaining quantity.

Set the original item's allowance quantity to zero.

Independent recalculation from split quantities and the Contract allowance percentage must not replace these values.

`_refresh_contract_over_billing_state()` must preserve the transferred allowances and the original item's zero value.

## Apply the rule to every allowance recalculation

If a Contract item has `block_item = 1` or `temp_block_item = 1`, set its allowance quantity to zero.

Apply this rule during VO refresh, invoice submission, and invoice cancellation.

For an active replacement, preserve the transferred allowance. Do not add the original allowance again during later recalculations.

Keep the existing calculation for unrelated active Contract items.

## Relevant files

Paths are relative to the `fabrica_construction` app root.

- `fabrica_construction/fabrica_construction/doctype/variation_order/variation_order.py`: rate-change split, original item blocking, and allowance refresh.
- `fabrica_construction/fabrica_construction/doctype/construction_invoice/construction_invoice.py`: invoice updates to paid and progress percentages and the later blocking transition.
- `fabrica_construction/services/contract_item_allowance.py`: shared lookup of transferred allowance identities for VO and invoice recalculation.
