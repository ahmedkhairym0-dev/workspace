# US-2026-11-0406: Rehandling Zero-Quantity Items in Construction Architecture

## Overview
Under the legacy architecture, zero-quantity items were segregated into dedicated child tables (`items_with_zero_qty`) on BOQ and Construction Contract, requiring specialized Variation Order flows ("Update Zero Qty Items") to move items between tables upon activation. 

In this redesigned single-table architecture, all items—whether positive or zero quantity—reside together within standard active child tables (`bill_of_quantity` on BOQ, `items` on Construction Contract, and `items` on Construction Invoice). Zero-quantity rows are retained without altering totals or accounting while quantity is zero, and they flow naturally through the lifecycle:
$$\text{BOQ} \longrightarrow \text{Construction Contract} \longrightarrow \text{Construction Invoice}$$

## Architectural Requirements & Specifications

### 1. Construction Settings
- Relabel setting `move_zero_quantity_items_on_submit` to **"Accept zero QTY"**.
- Preserve existing boolean state (0 or 1) and underlying schema fieldname.

### 2. Bill of Quantities (BOQ)
- **Single Child Table**: All items remain in `bill_of_quantity` (`tabBOQ Child`). Remove `items_with_zero_qty` field and separate `zero_qty_tab`.
- **Validation**:
  - When "Accept zero QTY" is enabled, rows with `qty == 0` are accepted and retained on submit.
  - Rows with `qty < 0` are strictly rejected.
  - Total calculations exclude contributions from zero-quantity rows (`amount = 0`).
- **Submission Lifecycle**: Decouple and remove `move_zero_qty_items(doc)`; no rows are transferred out of `bill_of_quantity`.

### 3. Construction Contract
- **Single Child Table**: All contract items reside in `items` (`tabConstruction Contract Items`). Remove `items_with_zero_qty` field and separate `zero_qty_tab`.
- **Mapping from BOQ**: Zero-quantity BOQ items map directly into `items` with `qty = 0`, `rate = boq_rate`, and `amount = 0`.
- **Validation & Pricing**: Zero-quantity items do not contribute to contract totals, taxes, or milestones.

### 4. Variation Orders (Subsystem Decommissioning)
- **Elimination of "Update Zero Qty Items" Flow**:
  - Removed "Update Zero Qty Items" option from `BOQ Variation Order` and `Variation Order` doctype option lists and client scripts.
  - Removed factory registration and flow class `flows/update_zero_qty_items.py`.
  - Removed controller hooks invoking `ZeroQtyContractVariation` and `ZeroQtyContractCancellation`.
  - Standard variation orders handle quantity or scope updates directly on active items.

### 5. Construction Invoice & General Ledger
- **Invoice Item Mapping**:
  - Rows originating from zero-contract-quantity items are mapped to `items` (`tabConstruction Invoice Items`) and tagged with `is_zero_qty_item = 1`.
- **Billing Rules**:
  - Invoiced quantity must be positive (`qty > 0`); entering `qty <= 0` is strictly rejected.
  - Calculated amount follows standard formula:
    $$\text{amount} = \text{qty} \times \text{rate} \times \text{paid}\% \times \text{progress}\%$$
  - Billed amount draws from the contract's shared over-billing allowance (`over_billing_allowance_remaining`).
- **Accounting & Back-Updates**:
  - Submitting an invoice generates balanced GL entries for all non-zero amounts.
  - Invoice submission updates `tabConstruction Contract Items` (invoiced quantity, invoiced amount, over-billing usage) and `tabBOQ Child`.
  - Invoice cancellation symmetrically reverses back-updates.

### 6. Legacy DocType and Table Deletions
The separate child table DocTypes and physical database tables have been dropped:
- Physical tables `tabBOQ Zero Qty Item` and `tabConstruction Contract Zero Qty Item` dropped via migration patch (`fabrica_construction.patches.v0_0_5.drop_zero_qty_item_doctypes_and_tables`).
- Metadata cleaned from `tabDocType`, `tabDocField`, `tabCustom Field`, and `tabProperty Setter`.
- DocType schema directories deleted from filesystem:
  - `fabrica_construction/fabrica_construction/doctype/boq_zero_qty_item/`
  - `fabrica_construction/fabrica_construction/doctype/construction_contract_zero_qty_item/`
- Obsolete utility modules deleted:
  - `fabrica_construction/utils/zero_qty_contract_variation.py`
  - `fabrica_construction/utils/zero_qty_contract_cancellation.py`
  - `fabrica_construction/utils/zero_qty_boq_reversal.py`
  - `fabrica_construction/utils/zero_qty_boq_variation.py`
  - `fabrica_construction/tests/boq/test_zero_qty_contract_mapping.py`

Active utilities `zero_qty_boq.py` and `zero_qty_contract.py` are streamlined to retain only settings initialization and contract upload source lookups directly against `tabBOQ Child`. All fallback queries to `tabBOQ Zero Qty Item` and `tabConstruction Contract Zero Qty Item` in `construction_invoice.py`, `zero_qty_contract.py`, and `taxes_and_totals.py` have been purged.

## Test Setup & Validation Matrix

### Setup Steps
1. In **Construction Settings**, enable **Accept zero QTY** and **Hide Dialog When Map BOQ to Construction Contract**.
2. Create test BOQ with mixed lines:
   - Item A: $\text{Qty} = 10$, $\text{Rate} = 100$
   - Item B (Zero Qty): $\text{Qty} = 0$, $\text{Rate} = 250$
3. Save and submit BOQ. Verify both rows remain in `bill_of_quantity`.
4. Create Construction Contract from BOQ. Verify both items map into `items`.
5. Create Construction Invoice from Contract:
   - Enter $\text{Qty} = 2$ for Item B.
   - Verify calculation: $\text{amount} = 2 \times 250 = 500$.
   - Submit invoice. Verify GL entries post and overbilling allowance is reduced by 500.
6. Cancel invoice. Verify overbilling allowance and contract item billed amounts revert cleanly.
