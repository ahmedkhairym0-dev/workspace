Objective
---------

Enhance the **Provisional Sum** behavior so the BOQ quantity is updated correctly based on how much quantity is taken into the Construction Contract.

This applies to the relationship between:

1.  BOQ
2.  Construction Contract
3.  Variation Order (VO)
4.  Invoice

Case 1: Full BOQ Quantity Taken in Contract
-------------------------------------------

When the user takes **all quantity of a BOQ Item** into the Contract and marks the Contract Item as **Provisional Sum**:

### Example

**BOQ:**

Item A

Qty = 100

Provisional Sum = No

**Contract:**

Item A

Qty = 100

Provisional Sum = Yes

### Expected Result

The entire BOQ quantity must be updated as Provisional Sum:

BOQ

Item A

Qty = 100

Provisional Sum = Yes

No additional BOQ row should be created.

Case 2: Part of BOQ Quantity Taken in Contract
----------------------------------------------

When the user takes **only part of the BOQ Item quantity** into the Contract and marks that Contract Item as **Provisional Sum**, the BOQ must split into two rows.

### Example

**Original BOQ:**

Item A

Qty = 100

Provisional Sum = No

**Contract:**

Item A

Qty = 40

Provisional Sum = Yes

### Expected Result in BOQ

Row 1

Item = Item A

Qty = 40

Provisional Sum = Yes

Row 2

Item = Item A

Qty = 60

Provisional Sum = No

The remaining quantity must be calculated as:

Remaining Qty = Original BOQ Qty - Contract Provisional Sum Qty

Case 3: Contract Item Quantity Is Split
---------------------------------------

When the user splits the quantity of an item in the Contract and marks one part as **Provisional Sum**, the BOQ must also be split accordingly.

### Example

**Original BOQ:**

Item A

Qty = 100

Provisional Sum = No

**Contract after split:**

Row 1

Item A

Qty = 40

Provisional Sum = Yes

Row 2

Item A

Qty = 60

Provisional Sum = No

### Expected Result in BOQ

The BOQ must reflect the same quantity split:

Row 1

Item A

Qty = 40

Provisional Sum = Yes

Row 2

Item A

Qty = 60

Provisional Sum = No

The BOQ quantity distribution must remain consistent with the Contract quantity distribution.

Invoice Validation in Variation Order
=====================================

When creating a **Variation Order**, the system must check whether the affected quantity already has an Invoice.

Expected Behavior
-----------------

If an Invoice exists for the item quantity, the user must **not** be allowed to mark that quantity as **Provisional Sum**.

### Example

Contract Item Qty = 100

Invoiced Qty = 40

The invoiced quantity:

Qty = 40

cannot be marked as Provisional Sum.

The system must block the change if the user tries to mark an already invoiced quantity as Provisional Sum.

### Validation Message

> **Cannot mark this item as Provisional Sum because part of its quantity has already been invoiced.**

General Rules
=============

1.  BOQ quantity must always remain consistent with Contract quantity.
2.  Marking a Contract Item as Provisional Sum must be reflected correctly in the BOQ.
3.  If the full BOQ quantity is Provisional Sum, mark the full BOQ row as Provisional Sum.
4.  If only part of the BOQ quantity is Provisional Sum, split the BOQ into separate rows.
5.  Remaining quantity must not be lost or duplicated.
6.  Splitting quantity in the Contract must result in the corresponding quantity split in the BOQ.
7.  A quantity that has already been invoiced cannot be converted to Provisional Sum through a VO.

Acceptance Criteria
-------------------

1.  **Full quantity:** Marking 100% of a BOQ item's contracted quantity as Provisional Sum marks the corresponding full BOQ quantity as Provisional Sum.
2.  **Partial quantity:** Marking only part of a BOQ item's quantity as Provisional Sum splits the BOQ into a Provisional Sum row and a remaining normal row.
3.  The sum of the split BOQ rows must always equal the original BOQ quantity.
4.  **Contract split:** Splitting an item quantity in the Contract must create the same corresponding quantity split in the BOQ.
5.  The Provisional Sum status must match the correct quantity row in the BOQ.
6.  No quantity may be duplicated or lost during BOQ splitting.
7.  In a VO, an item or quantity that already has an Invoice must not be allowed to be marked as Provisional Sum.
8.  The system must show a clear validation message when trying to mark invoiced quantity as Provisional Sum.
9.  Existing BOQ and Contract calculations must remain correct after saving, submitting, or updating the records.
