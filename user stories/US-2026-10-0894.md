For Retroactive Change Rate, the current VO header calculates totals from variation_quantity and variation_amount. Because the quantity stays unchanged, these totals become zero despite a changed rate. The contract also recalculates Variation Amount from quantity changes, omitting the rate-change effect.


Apply these rules specifically to Retroactive Change Rate:

- VO Total Quantity must sum the full quantities of included rows. Each row’s Amount must equal Quantity × New Rate, and monetary header totals must use those revised amounts, with the existing currency conversion, tax, discount and rounding rules.
- Keep revised contract amounts at Quantity × New Rate; do not add the rate difference again to those amounts. Rows marked Retroactivated remain excluded from contract totals under the separate exclusion requirement.
